(f) Activated-carbon electrodes with a three-layer alginate/PEDOT-containing hydrogel electrolyte, Ji et al., Fig. 1(d) [109]; this comparator contains no framework. (g) Na+ adsorption/expulsion with immobilized TFSI− in Ni3(HITP)2, Seyffertitz et al., Fig. 4 [99], for aqueous 1 M NaTFSI.
