EDITABLE FIGURES 1-18

Each Figure_N.pptx contains one slide and only that figure.

Editing panels
- Click a panel group to move or resize the panel and its identification together.
- Select the picture inside the group, or ungroup it (Ctrl+Shift+G), to crop or replace the image.
- Panel identifications in Figures 2-17 are editable PowerPoint text boxes.
- Use PowerPoint's Selection Pane to find the named panel groups and pictures.
- Add text, arrows, shapes, and other annotations using PowerPoint's normal tools.

What is editable
Figure 1: eight independently selectable numbered infographic regions and the central region. The central title is editable text. Other wording and illustrations remain embedded in the region pictures.
Figures 2-17: each lettered panel is an independent picture with an editable identification. Figure 2 includes separate m1-m4 objects. Figure 6 contains 12 panel groups, a-l. Figure 8 includes separate g1-g4 objects. Figure 15's final device demonstrations are a separate crop associated with panel q.
Figure 18: reconstructed with native PowerPoint text and shapes, grouped by numbered section. All diagram text and shapes can be edited.

Raster content and quality
Experimental photographs, micrographs, scientific diagrams, plot traces, axes, legends, and text inside the cropped raster panels retain the supplied image pixels. These internal features have NOT been reconstructed as editable chart data or individual drawing objects. The pictures themselves can be selected, moved, resized, cropped, and replaced independently.
The supplied PNG dimensions and aspect ratios are preserved. Crops are stored as lossless PNGs. Enlarging a picture cannot recover detail absent from the original attachment.
